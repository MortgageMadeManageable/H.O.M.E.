This is not intended to be something that people just mess with.  This is a passion project that I hope to bring into an industry where it is sorely needed.

  Long story short, I was in the process of buying a house, mainly the window shopping/pricing stage.  I'm the kind of person who wants to get a real accurate idea about the price range of something when external factors are at play.
  Unfortunately, for as popular and (maybe) useful as Zillow, redfin, or OneHome can be in locating a home and giving a "loose ballpark" for what you MIGHT spend.. What calculators/estimators they DO have are woefully insuffient.
  I would spend a few hours looking up houses, and then when I found a few that held my interest, I would have to grab the local data (mainly Zipcode+Extension) and do a manual search on the tax entities for that area just to see 
  what I was likely to pay in taxes, and none of those systems had the ability to even broadly adapt to things like Homestead, or veteran status/benefits.  And yeah, I get it.. they are serving the largest common denominator, not upset about it.
    That being said.  I DO/DID need that info, and I am a systems-person.  I realized there weren't really any lightweight, reliable apps that could provide a local and useful range for a persons monthly home payment.  Outside of
    certain very niche corporate calculator apps that have needless amounts of layering and signups.  So I created one from scratch.  The original one was with very very specific info related to my use-case.  It was actually my
realtor who, after seeing what I created, suggested I rewrite the app a bit to make something more broadly usable but with similar accuracy.  Thus, Welcome H.O.M.E. (Helpful One-Stop Mortgage Estimator) was born.  Maybe one day
this will become a marketable business solution, not sure yet.. This is my first real foray into coding since the days of HTML 2, but you just never know.
